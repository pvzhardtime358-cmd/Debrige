Mod Information

Debrige is A classic I, Zombie mod with many new & fun I, Zombie levels including minigames like Raining Zombies, Special Zombie Delivery, ETC & with a ton of variety of zombies to choose with special abilities.

  • Classic I, Zombie gameplay completely revamped
  • New & fun levels including minigames like Raining Zombies and Special Zombie Delivery
  • A ton of variety of zombies to choose from
  • Zombies equipped with unique and special abilities
  • Enhanced puzzle mechanics for endless strategy

Debrige is made for true PvZ fans who want to experience a nostalgic, yet deeply expanded, version of the I, Zombie mode!
